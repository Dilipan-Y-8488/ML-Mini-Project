# Synthetic Pancreatic Cancer Dataset (UE24CS352A)

## Dataset Disclaimer & Context
The clinical and radiomic data evaluated in the reference paper (*Arash Jamalian, "Pancreatic cancer prognosis using clinical and radiomic data", Stanford CS229, 2020*) originate from the Stanford Cancer Center / Quantitative Image Feature Extraction Pipeline (QFIP) and are protected under a strict Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA). Consequently, the raw patient cohort cannot be publicly redistributed.

To enable full computational reproducibility of the experimental machine learning workflow, the CSV files in this directory (`clinical_data.csv` and `radiomic_data.csv`) are **synthetic datasets** programmatically generated by `src/data_generator.py`. They are **NOT real patient data**. Experimental metrics obtained on this synthetic dataset are for educational demonstration and cannot be directly compared to or claimed to outperform the reference paper.

## Generation Methodology

The dataset is generated deterministically using NumPy's random number generator with fixed random seed `seed=42`:

### 1. Cohort Size & Latent Hazard Model
- **Cohort Size:** 80 synthetic patients (`STAN-PC-001` through `STAN-PC-080`).
- **Latent Risk Factor:** Patients are partitioned into a high-risk group ($n = 24$, survival $< 270$ days) and a low-risk group ($n = 56$, survival $\ge 270$ days), mimicking the class imbalance proportion observed in the reference paper's cohort.
- **Survival Days ($T$):**
  - High-risk cases: Simulated from a uniform distribution $\mathcal{U}(135, 266)$ days.
  - Low-risk cases: Simulated from a shifted Gamma distribution $\text{Gamma}(\text{shape}=2.4, \text{scale}=145.0) + 272$, clipped to $[273, 1285]$ days.
- **Latent Hazard ($\eta$):** Computed continuously from log-survival times:
  $$\eta = -\frac{\ln(T) - \mu_{\ln(T)}}{\sigma_{\ln(T)}}$$
  High positive values correspond to aggressive hazard, while negative values correspond to prolonged survival.

### 2. Clinical Features (12 Variables)
The clinical features are generated to correlate realistically with patient risk status and continuous survival:
1. `Stage_Grouping`: Categorical [0: Stage IB, 1: Stage IIA, 2: Stage IIB, 3: Stage III]. High-risk patients are weighted heavily toward Stages IIB (36%) and III (42%).
2. `Chemotherapy_Time`: Binary [0: Unchecked / No Post-SBRT Chemo, 1: Received Chemo after SBRT]. Strongly protective; 68% of low-risk patients received post-SBRT chemotherapy vs. 26% of high-risk patients.
3. `Radiation_Therapy`: Binary [0: SBRT, 1: Conventional Fractionation]. 70% of high-risk cases received conventional radiation.
4. `NCCN_Resectability`: Categorical [0: Resectable, 1: Borderline Resectable, 2: Locally Advanced]. Locally advanced disease dominates the high-risk cohort (55%).
5. `Tumor_Width_cm`: Continuous, $\mathcal{N}(3.9, 0.65)$ cm for high-risk vs. $\mathcal{N}(3.0, 0.70)$ cm for low-risk.
6. `Tumor_Height_cm`, `Tumor_Depth_cm`: Proportional dimensions with uniform variation $[0.75, 1.25]$ relative to width.
7. `Tumor_Volume_cm3`: Ellipsoidal tumor volume with additive Gaussian scatter, clipped to $[10.5, 99.5]\text{ cm}^3$ matching Figure 1.
8. `Tumor_Location`: Categorical [0: Head (62%), 1: Body (23%), 2: Tail (15%)].
9. `Primary_Tumor_Stage`: Ordinal T-stage [1: T1 to 4: T4], positively driven by latent hazard $\eta$.
10. `KPS_Score`: Karnofsky Performance Status, stepped in increments of 10 in $[60, 100]$, negatively correlated with hazard.
11. `Gender`: Binary [0: Female (48%), 1: Male (52%)].
12. `Survival_Days` & `Event_Observed`: Continuous target days and right-censoring indicator.

### 3. Censoring Mechanism
- **Right-Censoring Rate:** ~17.5% of the cohort ($14/80$ patients) are right-censored (`Event_Observed = 0`), sampled from patients surviving beyond 310 days, mimicking loss to follow-up or survival past study completion.

### 4. Radiomic Feature Matrix (900 Features)
Feature column names follow authentic PyRadiomics / Stanford QFIP naming conventions across first-order, shape, GLCM, GLSZM, and GLRLM families with 3D wavelet (LLL to HHH) and Laplacian-of-Gaussian (LoG-sigma 1, 3, 5 mm) filters:
- **8 Key Prognostic Features:** Indices 0–7 (including `wavelet-LLH_firstorder_Skewness`, `wavelet-HHH_glrlm_LongRunLowGrayLevelEmphasis`, etc.) carry explicit negative weights on latent hazard plus Gaussian noise ($\sigma = 0.82$).
- **482 Retained Signal Features:** Indices 8–489 feature structured block correlations (18 latent blocks) and moderate prognostic signal.
- **410 Collinear / High-Variance Features:** Indices 490–899 are constructed from 10 shared latent anchor components with very low noise ($\sigma = 0.012$), producing near-perfect collinearity and severe variance inflation ($SE(\beta) > 1.0$), mirroring the 410 unstable features pruned in the reference study.
